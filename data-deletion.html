Effective date: July 7, 2026

We do not maintain a permanent database of messages sent to our Facebook Page — your message content is used only in the moment to generate an automated reply and is not separately stored by us afterward. This means there is generally no data on our systems for us to delete beyond what Facebook/Meta itself retains as part of your Messenger conversation history.

How to delete your conversation history

You can delete your own copy of a Messenger conversation with our Page at any time directly within the Messenger app or Facebook, using Messenger's built-in "Delete Chat" or "Remove" options.

Requesting deletion from us

If you would like to formally request that we confirm no information about your interactions with our Page is retained on our systems, email us at ron.frenson.chua@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and the Facebook name or ID associated with your message. We will respond and confirm within a reasonable time.
