Privacy Policy

Precision Laser Machine Engravers Philippines
Effective date: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Precision Laser Machine Engravers Philippines ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you message our Facebook Page through Messenger.

What information we receive

When you send our Facebook Page a message, Meta's Messenger Platform shares the content of your message and your Facebook-assigned sender ID with our automated messaging system so that we can respond to you.

How we use this information

We use the content of your message only to generate an automated reply. Your message text is sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to help match your question to one of our prepared answers. We do not use your message content for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with any party other than the service providers described in this policy.

What we store

We do not maintain a permanent database of your messages. Message content is processed only in order to generate a reply and is not separately retained by us afterward. Facebook/Meta retains your conversation history according to their own policies, which you can review at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Third parties involved

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — operates the Messenger Platform used to deliver and receive messages.
  • Anthropic — provides the AI service (Claude API) used to help match your message to a relevant reply.

Your choices

You can stop messaging our Page at any time. To request deletion of any information associated with your interactions with our Page, see our Data Deletion Instructions.
